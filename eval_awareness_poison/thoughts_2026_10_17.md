Q: Canonical methods during SFT to get a language model _not_ to say certain things in response to certain inputs. Having a hard time giving "negative" sft signal here

A:

> 1. Positive replacement (the default). For each input where the model says the bad thing, write or sample the response you do want and SFT on that. Probability mass moves away from the bad completion only indirectly, because softmax is zero-sum. This works better than you’d expect, especially if the replacement diverges from the bad output early, since the first few tokens decide where the model ends up. Mix in plenty of nearby “should still answer normally” examples, or the model will learn an overly broad refusal.
